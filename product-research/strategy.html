Deliverable E · Purchase strategy

Three baskets, each a suggestion sized to the stated budget. Allocation logic: inventory is cash at the port (landed cost plus 18% import VAT, which is recovered on the next VAT return), samples and compliance are sunk test costs, advertising is the measured experiment, and contingency covers freight surprises, a failed sample round or a second supplier. Unspent money stays unspent — a larger budget does not have to be deployed in month one, and the scale basket deliberately leaves cash for re-orders of whichever SKU wins.

Selection rules: starter basket = passive products with MOQ ≤ 50, no standards, validated Israeli price; growth basket adds two higher-ticket SKUs with confirmed FOB and a bundle strategy; scale basket re-orders proven winners at better price tiers, adds branding/packaging, and reserves compliance money for one electrical SKU (thermal label printer) if the USB-only test proves demand.

Wholesale and private-label notes

  • Wholesale resale (to Israeli pet shops, baby shops, gadget stores) typically needs 2–2.5× landed to leave the retailer 40–50%: viable for the dish rack, stroller organizer, cooling mat and OBD lock at the modelled FOBs; not viable for the light bar or door lock at single-unit MOQs.
  • Private label: all five first-wave products are offered with OEM logo/packaging at MOQ 300–500 (Made-in-China and Global Sources listings); start with a Hebrew insert and sticker, move to printed boxes at the scale stage.
  • Organic vs paid: every product page shows contribution with and without ad cost; the dish rack, stroller organizer and sleep mask earn ₪40–64 per unit organically, which is why marketplace listings (Zap, KSP marketplace) and creator content matter more than cold Meta traffic.

Deliverable F · 30-day validation plan (top 5)

Principle: spend under ₪1,500 per product before any bulk order. Week 1 samples and supplier screening; week 2 Hebrew landing page and creative; weeks 3–4 a ₪1,000–1,500 ad test plus marketplace listing; day 30 stop/go. The success gate from the brief applies: positive contribution with ≥30 paid orders within 60 days inside the test budget.

#3 Roll-up over-the-sink silicone-coated dish drying rack

Sample ordering

  • Order 3 samples from two Global Sources/Made-in-China suppliers (304-steel + LFGB silicone spec; two colours), via express (~₪250 total).
  • Request the LFGB silicone report and the steel-grade certificate with the sample.

Quality inspection checklist

  • Count and weigh 100% of the sample lot; photograph every unit against the listing photos.
  • Check Hebrew label: importer name, address, country of origin, material/fibre content, care/safety text.
  • Drop/bend/stress test 3 units; log any failure and send photos to the supplier before paying the balance.
  • Measure extended length (must span 44–52 cm sinks); check rod-end caps for gaps; 24-hour wet test for rust; roll/unroll 50 times.

Supplier questions

  • Are you the manufacturer or a trading company? Factory address, business licence number, audit reports (BSCI/ISO 9001)?
  • Exact FOB price at 50 / 200 / 500 / 1,000 units, incoterm, payment terms (30/70 T/T, PayPal for samples), lead time and sample cost/credit.
  • Which certifications/test reports exist for this model (CE/RoHS/LFGB/EN71/REACH as relevant) and can we see the reports with the lab name?
  • Private label: logo print, Hebrew insert, custom box MOQ and cost; packaging dimensions and carton weight for freight quotes.
  • Defect policy: replacement or credit on DOA/defects reported within 30 days; warranty on materials.
  • Is the steel 304 or 201? Can you emboss or print our logo on the end caps / silicone?

Competitor price comparison

Observed Israeli prices: ₪34, ₪50, ₪60, ₪269, ₪279, ₪379. Planned test price ₪149 (override). Test at ₪149 single and ₪269 kosher pair (two colours).

Landing page test concept

One-page Hebrew store (Wix/Shopify) with a 15-second roll-out video over a small sink, a sink-width checker, kosher-pair selector and free pickup-point delivery over ₪199.

Small-budget marketing experiment

Two Meta video creatives (small-kitchen / kosher pair) at ₪60/day for 14 days plus a Zap listing; track add-to-cart and cost per order; retarget site visitors with the pair offer.
