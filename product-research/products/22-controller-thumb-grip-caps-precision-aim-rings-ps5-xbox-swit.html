1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Slippery/worn analog sticks, improved aim and stick-drift wear protection on expensive controllers
Who experiences it / frequency
Console gamers (teens/young adults), FPS players — Consumable: replaced every few months; multiple controllers per home
Current solutions & gaps
KontrolFreek (premium, $11-20) dominates; generic silicone caps $3-8; gaps: Hebrew-market bundles with controller skins/grips, gift packs
Target audience
Gamers 12-30, parents buying gifts
Why now
Cheap consumable with high repeat purchase and bundling value; too low ASP to carry shipping alone but strong as part of a gaming-accessory line.
