Skeptical review (Agent 7)
The cost basis is heavily overstated (fine for margin): B2B listings show $0.72-0.86 (MOQ 30), $0.90-1.20 (MOQ 100) and $0.99-2.99 (MOQ 1,000) versus $3.12 modelled. The bigger issue is the Israeli door: these locks need an inward-swinging door with a visible gap and a conventional strike plate; Israeli apartment doors are typically multi-point steel 'pladelet' doors with different strike geometry, and hotels increasingly use electronic locks - fit failure is the main return driver even in the US (reviews: 'requires visible gap', notch 'less versatile for thick gaps'). No Israeli comp exists (Zap only returns luggage travel locks at ~25-41 ILS and smart locks at 299-1,790), so 99 ILS for a ~$1 item has no anchor; 59-69 ILS is more plausible. IP: the closest patent found (US5480200, 1996) is expired; Addalock's own patents were not confirmed, so generic shapes are low risk.
Risk flags: fit on Israeli steel doors unknown; no Israeli comp; 99 ILS for ~$1 item invites marketplace undercutting; outward/flush doors incompatible
Model overrides applied
Very cheap to source but the use case (Israeli doors) is untested and there is no local price anchor; test fit on common Israeli door types before buying more than samples.
Import & regulatory risk summary
Low: passive metal part, no standard; duty modelled 6% (HS 8301). IP low (generic shape).
