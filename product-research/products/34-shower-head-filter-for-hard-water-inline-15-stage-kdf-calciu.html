1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Hard water (limescale) dulling hair/skin, clogging shower heads; chlorine odor
Who experiences it / frequency
Everyone on Israeli municipal water (notoriously hard, especially Jerusalem/center) — One-time + cartridge replacement every ~6 months (recurring revenue)
Current solutions & gaps
Whole-house softeners (expensive), vinegar descaling. Gap: cheap inline filter with recurring cartridges; efficacy claims are often exaggerated
Target audience
Women 25-45 (hair care), families with kids, Jerusalem/center residents
Why now
