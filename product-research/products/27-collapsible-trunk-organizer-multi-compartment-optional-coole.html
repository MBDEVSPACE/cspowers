Skeptical review (Agent 7)
Demand is solid and durable (Walmart 4.6 stars on 467 and 290 reviews; ACE carries the category) but the supplier basis is a single Global Sources knowledge page: real listings for a collapsible organizer with insulated cooler show $6.12-9.19 (MOQ 10), $11-13 (MOQ 100), $5-15 (MOQ 300, Cacces), $3-8 (MOQ 500, 2022 listing) and $2-5 (MOQ 1,000), so at the 100-300 units this budget allows the FOB is ~$6.5, not $4.15, and the carton is bulky. Israeli pricing supports 119: ACE sells Storex 28 L at 127 (159) and 40 L at 175, Zap lists Storex 3-compartment organizers at 54-93 (S/M/L) and a 60 L Sirius at 390-425; the file's 52-59 comps are small/seat-back items. A 40-56 L unit with cooler at 119 undercuts ACE's Storex while staying above the small Zap units. Complaints: collapses when empty, 'flimsy', durability of velcro base. No regulatory exposure; cooler liner should be declared food-safe only if marketed for food.
Risk flags: supplier price understated at small MOQ (~$6.5 with cooler); bulky carton -> freight cost per unit; ACE Storex at 127-175 with national distribution; collapses-when-empty / flimsy complaints
