1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Babies overheat in strollers/car seats in Israeli summer.
Who experiences it / frequency
Parents of infants/toddlers; very high birth rate in IL. — Seasonal daily use; often bought per child.
Current solutions & gaps
Hand fans, wet cloths. Gaps per reviews: flimsy 'octopus' arms snap, battery not lasting a day.
Target audience
Parents 25-40; grandparents as gift.
Why now
Massive proven demand and perfect for Israeli summer, but battery/SII burden and heavy competition (KSP, AliExpress) make margins thin; needs quality differentiation (blade guard, 10h battery).
