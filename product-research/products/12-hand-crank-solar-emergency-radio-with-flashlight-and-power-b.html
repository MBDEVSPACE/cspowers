1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Mamad / shelter preparedness: light, radio and phone charge without mains power.
Who experiences it / frequency
Every Israeli household (Home Front Command guidance), especially since 2023-2026 escalations — Standby; purchase spikes with security events
Current solutions & gaps
Battery flashlights, phone; crank radio covers all three; gaps: Israeli FM band support, Hebrew manual, cheap cranks break.
Target audience
Households, parents, elderly, gift for new apartment
Why now
Israel-specific recurring demand (shelters), high perceived value, good margins; battery flag is the main regulatory burden.
Israeli market notes
Hebrew: 'רדיו חירום', 'רדיו דינמו', 'רדיו ידני לממ"ד', 'ערכת חירום לממ"ד'. Israeli demand is event-driven and strong; KSP/Zap carry a few models. NOAA weather band irrelevant in IL - need FM 87.5-108.
