1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Dogs overheat on hot floors/balconies in summer; owners want a passive cooling surface for apartments and cars.
Who experiences it / frequency
Dog (and cat) owners in hot climates, apartment dwellers without AC running all day. — Seasonal daily use May-October in Israel; replaced every 1-2 seasons (punctures).
Current solutions & gaps
Wet towels, tile floors, AC. Gel mats are cheap but Amazon reviews cite punctures, gel smell and weak cooling; opportunity for thicker gel + washable cover bundle.
Target audience
Tel Aviv / Gush Dan dog owners 25-45, medium-large breeds.
Why now
Israeli summers (32–38 °C, May–September) and dense apartment dog ownership make a passive cooling surface an obvious seasonal buy; Israeli shelves carry Django/CAMON mats at ₪119–149 and 'coolers' at ₪150–215, so a ₪119 medium with a verified non-toxic gel and a Hebrew size guide competes on spec, not price.

Skeptical review (Agent 7)
The $0.77 supplier figure is a Global Sources snippet for the smallest 'ice pad' and understates a medium (65x50) pressure-gel mat; B2B snippets show $1.56-2.19 at 1000 pcs (ICE BEAR), $3.40-4.70 MOQ 100 and $2.80-3.99 MOQ 6, with the $0.76-0.78 tier at MOQ 500 for the small size. Use ~$2.50 for a medium. Israeli pricing is consistent with the file: Django/CAMON gel mats 119-149 by size, 'cooler' 150-215, Zap category 100-200; 119 ILS for a medium is fine. Demand is seasonal (May-Sept) and US-proxy; Israeli summer heat supports the category but inventory must land by April. Safety: ASPCA/AAHA warn that acrylamide-type gels in cooling pet beds/headache wraps cause neurologic toxicity when chewed; demand a gel SDS showing non-acrylamide, non-toxic gel and label 'not chew-proof, supervise'. No SII.
Risk flags: supplier $0.77 is small-size only; medium ~$2.50; strictly seasonal -> dead stock after September; gel toxicity if chewed (ASPCA warning) - require SDS; heavy per unit (gel) -> sea freight only
Model overrides applied
Price validated in Israel (119-215 range), no electrics, margin healthy even at $2.50 FOB for a medium; gate on a non-acrylamide gel SDS and plan a single seasonal buy.
Import & regulatory risk summary
Low regulatory burden (no SII); require gel SDS and 'not a toy' labelling; duty modelled 8%. Weight ~1 kg per medium — sea LCL preferred.
