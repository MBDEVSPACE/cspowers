1 · Product overview

Problem solved
High car-theft rates; visible deterrent that slows thieves even on keyless cars.
Who experiences it / frequency
Owners of commonly stolen models (Toyota, Mazda, Hyundai/Kia, Honda) in Israel. — Used daily at every parking
Current solutions & gaps
Immobilizers, GPS trackers (subscriptions), insurance-required anti-theft. Physical lock is cheap, no subscription; gaps: bulky, keys lost, cheap models cut easily.
Target audience
Car owners in high-theft areas, fleet/lease drivers, parents gifting
Why now
Durable, Israel-specific demand driver (theft), high perceived value, strong US sales data. Downsides: weight/shipping, unverified supplier pricing.
Israeli market notes
Hebrew: 'מנעול הגה', 'נעילת הגה', 'מנעול הגה נגד גניבה'. Israeli demand elevated by national car theft problem; many sellers on Zap but mostly unbranded. Weight (1-2.5kg) raises shipping cost.
