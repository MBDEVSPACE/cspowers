Skeptical review (Agent 7)
Demand evidence is generic trend copy ('home organization' in TikTok/Shopify guides) plus one TikTok Shop count (Sorbus 5,082 sold), i.e. hype-tinged, but lazy-susan organizers are an evergreen category so durability is acceptable. Supplier price $1.72 fits a single-tier plastic tray ($1.90-2.60 MOQ 2; $1.91-2.50 MOQ 10) but a 2-tier is $2.30 (MOQ 100) and 3-tier acrylic/gold $4.30-5.20 (MOQ 12), so $2.50 is the realistic median and shipping volume (tiers, acrylic breakage) matters more than FOB. The 129 ILS price is anchored to the branded comps; Israeli reality is Home Center's rotating 6-jar spice stand at 89.90, Zap's 'Mint' 6-jar at 83 (+19 shipping), ACE's large rotating stand at 111 (139), ACE's 50 cm bamboo turntable at 76-99 and a rotating makeup-brush organizer on Zap at 99. 89 ILS is the defensible price for a 2-tier cosmetics/snack turntable. Complaints are wobble/tipping with narrow bases and bearings that stiffen; food contact arises only if sold for loose spices/snacks (SI 5113 covers plastics and elastomers in contact with food), so market it for cosmetics/jars to avoid a declaration.
Risk flags: price 129 anchored to branded comps; mass market 83-111; bulky/fragile (acrylic) -> freight and breakage; wobble/tipping complaints with narrow bases; food contact only if marketed for loose spices/snacks (SI 5113); TikTok-driven demand evidence
Model overrides applied
Cheap, evergreen organizer with Israeli price validated at 83-111 for comparable items; test a 2-tier cosmetics version at 89 ILS with a wide base, and keep it out of direct food contact.
Import & regulatory risk summary
Low: no official standard expected; Hebrew labelling + importer details required.
