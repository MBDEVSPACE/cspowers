1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Cabin and steering wheel become unbearably hot in Israeli summer; conventional accordion shades are fiddly to fit and store.
Who experiences it / frequency
Every car owner parking outdoors; commuters in Gush Dan, Beer Sheva, Eilat. — Daily, May-October
Current solutions & gaps
Accordion/foil shades (cheap, poor fit, bulky); umbrella form factor folds small and deploys in seconds but quality varies (ribs break, loose fit).
Target audience
Car owners 25-60, parents, commuters
Why now
