The file's Israeli comps (10.2, 29.9) look like cable ties, not pouches; the real comps are Travelon Tech Accessory Organizer 89.90 and World Travel Essentials 119.90 at Lametayel, so 69 ILS undercuts the only branded options and could even be 79.
