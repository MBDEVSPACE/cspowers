The need is real (IKEA IL balcony-laundry page, 2,500-5,000 ILS dryers) but the recommended 259 ILS is above the category leader: Leifheit Classic 180 is 209-229 ILS at Home Center/Zap and the wall-mounted Leifheit 83201 ~195; CLEANIC wall-folding rack 69.90-79.90; a 3-stage zigzag aluminium wall rack 189; ACE aluminium folding ~113 on promo. An unbranded aluminium wall/railing rack must land at 149-179 ILS. Supplier $10.48 matches B2B ($4.99-10.99 MOQ 10; $9.45-10.35 MOQ 100; $9.50-10.90 MOQ 37). No railing-mounted model was found at any Israeli retailer - possibly a gap, but also no evidence Israelis buy that format (balcony railings vary: glass, bars, concrete parapet), so a railing clamp is a fit-risk. Long, heavy cartons raise freight; aluminium quality and hinge pins are the usual failure points. No SII standard.
