Demand is durable (URPOWER 46,000+ reviews, ~8K/month) and the supplier estimate of $10 is actually conservative: Global Sources lists a 137x147 cm 600D hammock at ~$7.3 FOB (MOQ 1), Alibaba 600D at $7-10.27 (MOQ 5), and 500-piece MOQs at $2-4. The problem is the Israeli price: ACE sells a back-seat dog cover at 71 ILS (88 regular), InnovaGoods at 99, an Oxford cover with pockets and boot cover at 199, Max Stock at 30 ILS, and Zap lists 129-164 ILS (189 for a full 'bathtub' style). 219 ILS is above every comp found; 149-169 is the ceiling for an unbranded hammock. Quality complaints are consistent across brands: buckles/straps break, cover slides, thin fabric not waterproof, zips fail - specify metal buckles, 600D+TPU and anti-slip backing. Bulky pack size raises freight per unit.
Risk flags: price above all Israeli comps; strap/buckle failure complaints; bulky freight; Max Stock 30 ILS low-end anchor
Model overrides applied
Good margin even at 159 ILS (FOB ~$7-8) and proven demand; test 50-100 units with upgraded buckles and check fit on Israeli compact sedans/SUVs.
Import & regulatory risk summary
Low: no official standard expected; Hebrew labelling + importer details required.
