Skeptical review (Agent 7)
Demand is modest but durable (stick ~6K/month US; OutdoorGearLab maintains a ranking); no Israeli data. Supplier price US$1.15 is for a bare stick: B2B shows handheld sticks at US$0.99-1.99 (MOQ 200), US$1.60-2.00 (MOQ 100), a 45.7 cm stick at US$2.54 (MOQ 20) and 45 cm colour sticks at US$2.50-3.50 (MOQ 1000); with a spiky ball the bundle is ~US$2.2. Israeli anchors: Decathlon sells a 3-in-1 kit (roller + ball + stick) at 99.90 and a roller/ball/300 mm stick kit at 129.90, a smooth roller at 49.90; Zap lists a telescopic roller massager at 54 and a metal manual roller at 97; the file's 21.33 comp is a marketplace stick. A stick + ball at 59 ILS sits below Decathlon's kits and above AliExpress, which is a defensible slot. Complaints are mild: beads that lock or slip, bruising from hard ridges, one report of a toxic smell from PVC handles (ask for phthalate-free PVC/TPR). No SII, no fit returns; low capital.
Risk flags: supplier_usd understated for the bundle; Decathlon kits at 99.90-129.90 include a roller; PVC odour / material compliance; bruising complaints with hard beads; no Israeli demand data
