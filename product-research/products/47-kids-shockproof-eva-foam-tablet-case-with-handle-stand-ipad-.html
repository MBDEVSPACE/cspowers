1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Children drop tablets; parents need a grippable, bouncy case with a stand for car trips and screen time
Who experiences it / frequency
Parents of kids 2-10; grandparents — One-time per tablet; re-buy on new tablet; colors drive second purchase
Current solutions & gaps
AVAWO, BMOUO, imoshion, Dteck; gaps: Israel-specific size coverage (Samsung Tab A9 very common in Israel), Hebrew gift packaging, bundled glass protector
Target audience
Parents 28-45
Why now
Consistent demand with 'best seller' badges and 200-300+ review SKUs; light, non-fragile, very demonstrable (drop test video); Israel has many Samsung Tab A-series kids' tablets with limited local case choice.

6 · Regulatory, IP and risk

Israeli standard (SII) likely required
possible Children's product (not a toy, but used by kids): EVA foam should have REACH/phthalate-free test reports; SII toy standard (SI 562) could be argued for under-3 use - market for ages 3+ and label accordingly
Flags
children product ·
IP / design-right risk
low Generic handle-case shapes; avoid character/Disney prints and Apple/Samsung logos [QA review set level: low]
Return / quality risk class
medium (return reserve 5%)
Skeptical review (Agent 7)
Demand is real but US-proxy (Walmart 'best seller' badges with 226-335 reviews) and the category is model-fragmented: iPad 10.9 (10th gen), iPad 11 (A16), iPad Air 10.9 and Samsung Tab A9/A11 are different moulds, so a 20-50k ILS budget would be split across 4-6 SKUs with wrong-model returns. Supplier price of $5.61 is roughly right: Alibaba shows a kids EVA stand case for the 10th-gen/A16 at $3.49-3.94 (MOQ 50) and $3.60 (MOQ 50); Made-in-China trading listings are $6.40-6.60 (MOQ 10) and $8.00-8.15 for the Air mould. The 149 ILS price is anchored to the top comp; Israeli mass market is Ivory's own-brand kids case for iPad 10.9/A16 at 99 ILS (the same page also shows 79 and 139), Ivory's Samsung Tab A8 kids case with handle at 49 ILS, Bug's Oukitel OT6 kids case at 99, and plain Zap silicone cases at 49-149. KSP lists no handle case at all, so the online channel is open but Ivory's house brand sets the ceiling. Regulatory: SII says SI 562 covers toys up to age 14 and phthalate testing applies to soft-PVC toys/stationery; an EVA tablet case is not a toy, so 'possible' stands only if it is marketed for under-3s; keep REACH/phthalate reports on file and label 3+. IP low (generic handle shapes; no character prints).
Risk flags: model-fragmented: 4-6 moulds needed -> capital split and wrong-model returns; Ivory house-brand kids case at 49-99 ILS; price 149 unsupported; children's product: keep REACH/phthalate report, label 3+; US demand proxies only
