1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Dangling power strips and cable spaghetti under standing/home desks; tidy look and vacuum-friendly floor
Who experiences it / frequency
Home-office workers, gamers, standing-desk owners — One-time per desk; strong attach rate to standing-desk purchases
Current solutions & gaps
Scandinavian Hub, PAMO, Cinati lead on Amazon ($14-20); IKEA SIGNUM in Israel; gaps: clamp-on (no drilling) versions for rented apartments, black/white, longer 17in trays
Target audience
Home office / gaming setup owners 25-45
Why now
Strong WFH/standing-desk-driven demand, little Israeli local competition, satisfying before/after video content; passive with zero regulatory burden. Watch weight (~0.9 kg) and box size for shipping cost.

Skeptical review (Agent 7)
Commodity with real but shallow demand (ASIN trackers 3-4K/month at $14-20). Supplier price $4.57 matches B2B listings ($2.90-4.00 at MOQ 5; $3.50 at MOQ 200; $4.50-4.80 at MOQ 50). Israeli comps are denser than the file shows: Keisar cable tray 119 ILS at Bug and 89 ILS incl. shipping on Zap; LUMI clamp tray 159-168; cable sleeves/tubes 25-145 at Home Center/Ivory/ACE. 159 ILS is the top of the local range for a no-name tray; 119-129 is safer. Complaints: sharp edges, missing screws, clamp range not fitting thick desks, tray too shallow for a power strip. Heavy steel increases freight; packaging must protect finish. No SII.
Risk flags: commodity, thin differentiation; Keisar at 89-119 ILS; sharp edge / missing-parts complaints; freight weight
