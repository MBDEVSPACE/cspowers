1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Dogs overheat on hot floors/balconies in summer; owners want a passive cooling surface for apartments and cars.
Who experiences it / frequency
Dog (and cat) owners in hot climates, apartment dwellers without AC running all day. — Seasonal daily use May-October in Israel; replaced every 1-2 seasons (punctures).
Current solutions & gaps
Wet towels, tile floors, AC. Gel mats are cheap but Amazon reviews cite punctures, gel smell and weak cooling; opportunity for thicker gel + washable cover bundle.
Target audience
Tel Aviv / Gush Dan dog owners 25-45, medium-large breeds.
Why now
Strong Israel climate fit, very easy video demo (dog flops on mat), low regulation, cheap unit cost; seasonal and gel-puncture returns are the main risks.
