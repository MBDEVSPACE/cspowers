Problem solved
Cats drink too little -> urinary issues; stagnant bowls.
Who experiences it / frequency
Cat owners, especially of older/indoor cats. — Daily; filters are consumable (monthly).
Current solutions & gaps
Plain bowls, dripping taps. Gaps: pump cleaning hassle, noise, plastic odor.
Target audience
Cat owners 25-50.
Why now
Huge demand and filter repeat revenue, but electrical compliance (adapter) and warranty/pump failures raise burden; consider only if supplier has CE/UL test reports.

6 · Regulatory, IP and risk

Israeli standard (SII) likely required
possible FLAG ELECTRICAL: USB 5V pump with a mains USB adapter - if adapter is included it needs SII/CE approval; ship without adapter or with certified adapter. Water + electricity = liability.

Return / quality risk class
medium (return reserve 5%)
Skeptical review (Agent 7)
Demand is strong (Veken ~30K/month) and supplier price $11.2 sits inside the B2B band ($2.5-9.9 MOQ 100 Joypaws; $10-15 MOQ 200 Maxtop; $15.7 MOQ 1,000). But the Israeli price of 229 ILS is above branded fountains: Trixie 1 L 159, Catit 2 L 175, 1.6 L stainless-look 199, Catit Flower 3 L 249 (all on Zap). An unbranded unit must sit at ~169 ILS. This is the only electrical item in the top 18: a 5 V DC pump in water plus a mains USB adapter. If an adapter ships in the box it is a power supply that needs SII type approval before customs release (the SII importer guide confirms listed products need an 'אישור ת״ר' - the specific adapter standard could not be verified by search; treat as required). Ship without adapter or source an SII-approved one locally. Quality: pump failure at 4-14 months, mould, replacement pumps unavailable - warranty returns will be high unless spare pumps are stocked.
Risk flags: USB adapter = SII approval if bundled; water + electricity liability; pump failure / mould complaints; branded comps 159-249 ILS; filter consumables logistics
