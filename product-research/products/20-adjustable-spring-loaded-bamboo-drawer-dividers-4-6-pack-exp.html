Skeptical review (Agent 7)
The supplier basis is a Walmart retail price x0.6, not a B2B quote. B2B listings show $1.50-3.25 per divider (Alibaba tiered $3.25 at 500 down to $1.52 at 5,000; Global Sources $2.40 at 40 pcs), so a 6-pack is ~$9-15 FOB; $16.19 is tolerable but should be re-quoted at ~$12. The 329 ILS retail is not supported by anything in Israel: no spring-loaded divider set was found at Zap/ACE/Home Center/Max; the nearest items are an expandable bamboo cutlery tray at 79.90 ILS (Home Center) and ACE expandable trays at ~79.90. A 6-pack at 149-179 ILS is the realistic ceiling. Complaints: spring tension too weak (dividers wobble/shift), length shorter than advertised (the file's own Walmart quote). 55 cm length means long cartons, higher freight, and bamboo mould risk in sea freight.
Risk flags: cost basis is retail x0.6; no Israeli comp; spring weakness / size complaints; long carton freight, mould risk
Model overrides applied
No Israeli price anchor and a retail-derived cost basis; re-quote FOB and test spring strength before buying, and reprice to ~169 ILS.
