Skeptical review (Agent 7)
The SKU is a bundle (5 loop bands + tube set with door anchor) but the $2.10 supplier price is for the loop set alone (Jiangsu Meilekang $2.10 at 500-999 sets with REACH; TPE $1.60-2.00 MOQ 500; $1.40-1.60 MOQ 200); tube sets with handles/ankle straps/door anchor list at $4.30-5.96 (MOQ 50) and $5.81 (MOQ 500), so the bundle FOB is ~$7. Israeli pricing is dense: loop sets 54-79 (Apex 54 incl. shipping, TheraBand loop 59 on Ivory, 79 set on Zap), 5-band handle sets 35-74, 11-piece tube sets 69-120 and ACE's MD BUDDY pro set with door anchor at 199; Decathlon also stocks bands. A combined bundle at 69 ILS is underpriced against its own contents; 119 is the right bundle price (or split: loops 59, tubes 89). Safety is the new finding: resistance-band snap injuries have produced US product-liability suits (Harry Reid v. Thera-Band; a door-anchor ball propelled into a user's eye in Brooklyn v. BRG Sports), so warnings, an anchor load rating (>=90 kg) and latex-allergy labelling are mandatory and liability insurance should be checked. Demand is huge and durable (Fit Simplify ~30K/month, 136K reviews) but saturated.
Risk flags: supplier price covers loop set only; bundle ~$7 FOB; snap/door-anchor eye-injury litigation precedent -> warnings, insurance; latex allergy labelling; saturated: Decathlon, ACE, Ivory, Zap sellers at 35-199; chemical-smell complaints on unboxing
