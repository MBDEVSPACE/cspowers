1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Nowhere to put phone, keys, bottle while pushing a stroller.
Who experiences it / frequency
Parents of 0-3 year olds. — One per stroller; gifted at baby showers.
Current solutions & gaps
Diaper bags, hooks. Gaps: slipping, no phone pocket.
Target audience
Parents 25-40; gift buyers.
Why now
Israel's birth rate (about 3 children per woman) keeps stroller accessories evergreen; Momcozy holds #1 in Amazon stroller organizers with 21k+ reviews; Israeli shelves are polarised between Infanti unbranded at ₪59–88 and boutique brands (Minene ₪140, Gitta ₪199, Bugaboo ₪300–329), leaving a ₪99 slot for a well-made private-label unit.

Demand citations are US (Momcozy #1 with 21k reviews) and Walmart listings with 16-17 reviews; three of four 'suppliers' in the file are Western retail (eBay.de 21.99, Babylist 35, Currys) and must be ignored. B2B snippets confirm $2.10-4.45 at 300-1000 pcs (Runhong $2.10 MOQ 300; JingHao $3.10-4.20 MOQ 500; Magikids $4.45 at 50-999) so $3.57 is fine; note MOQs of 300-500 per style. Israeli pricing: Infanti universal organizer 59-88 on Zap, Minene 139.93 (199.90 list, out of stock) at Terminal X, Gitta 199 at ACE, Bugaboo 300-329; a plain cup holder is 35-39. The file's 119 sits between the unbranded Infanti and the boutique brands; 99 ILS is safer. Complaint pattern: straps that do not grip some stroller handles and organizer sliding, i.e. fit-dependent returns; cup-holder durability with large bottles.
Risk flags: 3 of 4 file 'suppliers' are Western retail, not FOB; MOQ 300-500 per style; strap fit varies by stroller -> returns; Infanti unbranded at 59-88 caps price
Model overrides applied
Generic textile accessory with healthy margin at 99 ILS and a real gap between Infanti (59-88) and boutique brands (140-330); test with 300 units and publish a stroller-compatibility list to limit 14-day returns.
Import & regulatory risk summary
Low: textile accessory; Hebrew label; children's-product caution (no small detachable parts claims). Duty modelled 6%; textiles could be 12% — confirm HS 4202/6307.
