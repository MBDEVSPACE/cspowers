1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Monitor sits too low causing neck strain; small desks lack storage for keyboard/notebooks.
Who experiences it / frequency
Hi-tech and hybrid workers with home desks, students, anyone using a laptop with an external screen. — Daily use; one-time purchase per desk (replaced when moving / upgrading setup).
Current solutions & gaps
Books/boxes under the monitor; branded Fellowes/Keisar risers in Israel at ₪149+ are plain plastic/MDF without drawers; IKEA has limited choice. Gap: affordable riser with drawer + phone slot under ₪130.
Target audience
WFH professionals 25-45, students, small-office buyers (multi-unit).
Why now
Durable evergreen demand (10K/mo top ASIN, +100% MoM for drawer variant) and thin Israeli selection, but volumetric weight (~1.2 kg, bulky) compresses margin on sea/air freight; works best as a hero SKU in a WFH bundle.
Israeli market notes
Few dedicated riser-with-drawer SKUs seen on Bug/Home Center; ₪62-149 price band for plain risers leaves room for a ₪99-139 riser with drawer.
