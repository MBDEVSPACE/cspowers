Problem solved
Wrist pain / RSI from long typing and mouse sessions; keyboards without palm rests
Who experiences it / frequency

Gorilla Grip, Gimars, TECKNET, HyperX, Fellowes ($10-25); gaps: TKL/75% sizes matching mechanical keyboards, leather-look sets at ILS 59-89
Target audience
