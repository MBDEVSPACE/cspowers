1 · Product overview

Problem solved
Beach towels and cotton blankets trap sand and stay wet; a sand-shedding ripstop/mesh blanket with corner stakes and pockets stays clean, dries fast and packs small.
Who experiences it / frequency
Beach-goers, families with kids, picnic/park users — Weekly in Israeli beach season (Apr-Oct)
Current solutions & gaps
Towels, cotton blankets, straw mats; gaps: sand trapping, wind lift, bulk. Cheap mats tear and have weak stakes.
Target audience
Tel Aviv/Haifa/Eilat beach families, 25-45, gift buyers before summer
Why now
Strong, broad US demand (multiple ASINs ~10K/mo, 15K-review leader), lightweight, cheap to source ($3.5-7 est.), perfect fit for Israeli beach culture and video demo (pour sand, shake off). Seasonal.
