Skeptical review (Agent 7)
Israel-native demand exists (Zap has a dedicated scooter phone-holder category; e-scooter injuries 1,469 in 2022) but the regulatory climate is turning against scooters: licence plates became mandatory on 1.8.2024 with very low initial registration (1,082), and a July 2026 Or Yarok survey found most Israelis favour outlawing e-scooters, so the installed base could shrink. The supplier price $2.15 is for a basic clamp: anti-vibration mounts with dampeners list at $2.70-3.75 (MOQ 100), $4.89-5.52 (MOQ 10), $4.00-4.10 (MOQ 1,000, GoalLock) and quad-lock-style at $7.04-7.64, so ~$4 is the right FOB for the 'anti-vibration locking' spec. Israeli prices: Ivory's own-brand bike/scooter mounts at 65-69, GUB PRO1 79-119, Xiaomi 119, Kewig M8SP 129, generic metal 99, Zap's 50-100 band; 69 is therefore at Ivory's house-brand level and 79 is only justified with a real dampener. IP: Quad Lock lists AU/CN/EP patents and threatens enforcement on its dual-stage lock; design patent D905041 cites Quad Lock and Rokform twist-locks as prior art, so avoid twist-lock geometry (medium). Liability angle: Apple's support note says scooter/moped vibration can damage OIS/autofocus and recommends a dampening mount; buyers whose cameras fail will blame the mount.
Risk flags: Ivory house-brand scooter/bike mounts 65-69 ILS; Quad Lock/Rokform twist-lock patents -> avoid that mechanism; handlebar-diameter / phone-size fit -> returns; e-scooter regulatory climate (plates, outlaw proposals) -> demand risk; OIS camera-damage claims if dampener is ineffective
